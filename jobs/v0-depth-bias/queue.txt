# v0-depth-bias clears its D32_SFLOAT depth attachment to 0.5 and draws geometry
# that starts at that same depth, so the unbiased frame fails LESS against the
# equal clear and keeps no pixel. The frames that follow measure the six words
# of ps5-opengl's PA_SU_POLY_OFFSET_* block: each sign of the constant factor
# (LESS with a bias toward the viewer, GREATER with one away from it), the slope
# factor on a ramp, and that slope capped by depthBiasClamp. Every frame's
# pixels and its depth plane are read back, so the words are proved by what the
# hardware did rather than by the draw being accepted
# (docs/M5_PHASE_C.md, R1). m2-solid and v0-cull are the regressions behind it.
